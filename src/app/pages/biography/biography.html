Pascal Koomson

A Voice for the Kingdom

The Journey

Pascal Koomson is a prolific gospel recording artist and worship leader whose ministry has transcended borders, touching lives across the globe. Born and raised with a deep-seated passion for the divine, his musical journey began in the local church where he discovered the power of a single note to shift atmospheres.

With a unique blend of African rhythmic intensity and contemporary soulful melodies, Pascal has carved a niche as a minister who doesn't just perform, but creates an altar of worship wherever he stands. His commitment to excellence is seen not only in his vocal prowess but in his integrity as a servant of God.

Artistry & Ministry

Beyond the music, Pascal is the visionary behind "Pascal Koomson Ministries," an organization dedicated to raising a generation of spirit-filled ministers. Through initiatives like the "Sing Out Experience" and "Choose Jesus Campaign," he continues to mentor young artists and lead evangelistic outreaches to the unreached.
